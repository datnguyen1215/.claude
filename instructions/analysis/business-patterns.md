# Business Analysis Patterns

## Revenue Reality Framework

### The 70% Rule

Apply decisions when reaching 70% certainty. Waiting for perfect information leads to missed opportunities. Act decisively with reasonable confidence rather than pursuing complete certainty.

### Unit Economics Focus

Analyze every feature and decision through unit economics:

- Evaluate cost per user acquisition
- Measure lifetime value impact
- Calculate contribution margin changes
- Assess payback period implications
- Determine break-even points

### Revenue Impact Assessment

Before implementing any feature or change:

1. Identify direct revenue impact
2. Quantify indirect revenue effects
3. Evaluate opportunity costs
4. Consider revenue timing implications
5. Assess sustainability of revenue model

## Minimalist Metrics Approach

### Core Metric Selection

Maintain focus on metrics that directly influence business outcomes:

- Revenue growth rate
- Customer acquisition cost
- Customer lifetime value
- Gross margin
- Burn rate and runway

### Metric Hierarchy

Establish clear metric priorities:

1. Primary metrics that determine survival
2. Secondary metrics that indicate health
3. Vanity metrics to avoid entirely

### Measurement Discipline

When establishing metrics:

- Define clear measurement criteria
- Set actionable thresholds
- Establish review cadences
- Automate data collection
- Eliminate metrics that don't drive decisions

## Feature Evaluation Framework

### Revenue Justification

Every feature must answer:

- How does this generate revenue?
- What is the expected return on investment?
- What is the implementation cost?
- What is the opportunity cost?
- Can existing features achieve similar results?

### Priority Matrix

Evaluate features using:

- Revenue impact (high/medium/low)
- Implementation effort (high/medium/low)
- Time to revenue (immediate/short-term/long-term)
- Strategic alignment (core/supporting/peripheral)

### Feature Rejection Criteria

Reject features that:

- Lack clear revenue connection
- Require disproportionate resources
- Distract from core value proposition
- Increase complexity without commensurate value
- Serve wants rather than needs

## Business Model Analysis

### Revenue Stream Evaluation

Assess each revenue stream for:

- Scalability potential
- Margin characteristics
- Customer stickiness
- Competitive defensibility
- Growth trajectory

### Cost Structure Optimization

Continuously evaluate costs:

- Identify largest cost drivers
- Find reduction opportunities without quality loss
- Automate repetitive expensive processes
- Negotiate better terms with vendors
- Eliminate non-essential expenses

### Pricing Strategy

Apply pricing decisions based on:

- Value delivered to customer
- Competitive positioning
- Cost structure requirements
- Market willingness to pay
- Revenue optimization potential

## Decision Making Framework

### Quick Decision Criteria

For rapid decisions, evaluate:

- Revenue impact within 90 days
- Implementation cost under threshold
- Reversibility of decision
- Learning value from experiment

### Investment Evaluation

For significant investments:

- Calculate expected return
- Assess risk-adjusted value
- Consider alternative uses of resources
- Evaluate strategic importance
- Set clear success metrics

### Pivot Indicators

Recognize when to change direction:

- Consistent failure to meet revenue targets
- Unsustainable unit economics
- Market rejection signals
- Competitive disruption
- Resource depletion timeline

## Continuous Optimization

### Regular Review Cycles

Establish routine evaluations:

- Weekly revenue tracking
- Monthly unit economics review
- Quarterly strategy assessment
- Annual model validation

### Rapid Experimentation

Test assumptions quickly:

- Design minimal viable tests
- Set clear success criteria
- Time-box experiments
- Make decisions on data
- Iterate based on results
